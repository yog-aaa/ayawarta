# Siger Priangan T1 image generation prompts

Six PNG files were generated with Codex's built-in `image_gen` tool. The two v2 compositions used their v1 counterparts as edit references; no third-party reference image was supplied. The v1 compositions are retained for comparison but superseded because their floral balance is generic and their `Akad Nikah` label conflicts with the neutral-default brief. These are exploratory studies for review, not runtime assets or evidence of a historically exact bridal ornament. One rejected desktop output with invented people and incorrect sample dates was discarded; it is not part of this board.
